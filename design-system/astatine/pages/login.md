# Login Page Overrides

> **Project:** Astatine
> **Page type:** Authentication

Use the Master claymorphism palette and typography. Keep the sign-in flow
focused, with a value-proposition panel beside the form on wide screens and a
stacked, scrollable layout on narrow screens.

## Layout

- Constrain the desktop composition to a centered, wide card with a two-column
  layout; stack the hero and form below 900 px.
- Keep email and password fields, language selection, error feedback, and
  account creation available at narrow widths.
- Allow password managers and paste; provide visible keyboard focus.

## Components and states

- Use the login artwork as a decorative hero background with a legible overlay.
- Use a prominent orange CTA against the indigo/cream palette.
- Morph the CTA into a compact progress indicator while signing in, shake the
  form on authentication failure, and announce errors inline.
- Preserve the selected app theme and Vietnamese/English copy.
